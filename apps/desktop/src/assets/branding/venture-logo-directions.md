# Investor presentation logo directions

Three new creative directions, generated with the built-in image generation tool. The aim is a distinctive financial technology identity for investor presentations, with code and candlestick motifs integrated into the symbols. These are visual concepts for selection, not claims about investor response. Each PNG has an opaque navy background. Earlier concepts are preserved; the app interface and operating-system icons have not been changed.

## 1. Nightwing

[PNG](budget-terminal-option-4-nightwing.png)

Final generation prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create a daring, exceptionally refined original identity for "Budget Terminal", a financial intelligence software startup presenting to venture capital investors. This is a creative reset: the aim is an ownable, instantly memorable technology brand with the craft of a top independent identity studio.
Concept NIGHTWING: design an abstract bilateral mark that at first reads as a precise winged instrument, almost a moth seen as an engineered glyph, but is actually constructed from financial candlestick geometry and code. Three tapered vertical candlestick bodies with fine projecting wicks on each side fan outward from a central diagonal code slash. Deliberate cutouts subtly form opening and closing programming brackets. Two or three short offset horizontal code-stroke cuts nest into the lower wings. Use bold masses and generous negative space, not a delicate illustration. It must feel like one compact surprising symbol, not a moth drawing, no insect anatomy, no antennae, no face, no literal chart or screen.
Render the symbol in a single vivid ice mint #a9f3d0 on a perfectly uniform near-black navy #080b12 field. To the right set exact text "Budget Terminal" in an elegant confident contemporary grotesk, normal width, medium weight, title case, pale ivory #f1f0e9. Carefully tailored letter spacing, strong visual balance.
Wide horizontal logo canvas with generous empty margins. One symbol and one wordmark only, large and clean. Flat vector-like solid-color design, pristine sharp edges. No financial clipart, no growth arrow, no coins, no dollar sign, no BT monogram, no rounded app tile, no terminal window, no slogan, no labels, no extra text, no mockup, no glow, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no 3D, no watermark. Venture technology identity, original and disciplined rather than decorative.
```

## 2. Continuum

[PNG](budget-terminal-option-5-continuum.png)

Final generation prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create one highly distinctive premium logo for "Budget Terminal", a financial intelligence software startup aiming for the credibility of a venture-backed infrastructure platform. Sophisticated independent brand studio quality.
Concept CONTINUUM: an angular impossible loop made from one folded ribbon, seen straight on as a compact asymmetric architectural knot. It is NOT a generic infinity sign: make it a sharply folded triangular three-sided circuit with one unexpected open cut, distinctive silhouette and bold negative space. Along one short ribbon edge, three elegant candlestick-like vertical cuts with thin protruding wicks reveal market data as a structural detail. Along the opposite folded edge, three staggered short parallel code-line cutouts and a small bracket-shaped gap suggest source code. These motifs must be inseparable from the geometry, not pasted on. Use flat solid geometric facets in cobalt #467eff and pale periwinkle #a4b6ff, no gradients or 3D shading.
To the right render exact words "BUDGET TERMINAL" in a finely crafted neo-grotesk uppercase wordmark, medium weight, clean understated typography, two lines left aligned with consistent tracking, pale silver #e7edf5. Uniform midnight navy #080b12 background. Wide horizontal composition, emblem not larger than the wordmark block, generous clear padding and excellent optical alignment.
Only one finished logo. Absolutely no literal chart, no upward arrow, no square computer frame, no BT letters as icon, no currency sign, no coin, no tiny numbers, no mascot, no generic fintech clipart. No slogan, no labels, no extra text, no watermark, no mockup, no shadows, no textures, no glow, no gradients, no 3D rendering. Crisp vector-style identity. Original, confident, memorable.
```

## 3. Signal Weave

[PNG](budget-terminal-option-6-signal-weave.png)

Final generation prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create an unusually memorable, beautifully crafted logo for "Budget Terminal", a serious financial intelligence technology startup pitching venture capital investors. Avoid stock finance icons. The logo should look commissioned from an inventive top identity studio.
Concept SIGNAL WEAVE: construct a compact abstract emblem from three thick horizontal source-code strokes that interlace with three vertical market candlestick strokes, producing one surprising woven glyph. Each horizontal stroke starts or ends in an angular curly-brace-like turn; each vertical stroke has a substantial body and a fine projecting wick. Interlocking over/under negative-space cuts make the two systems feel like one tightly engineered material. Bold clean strokes, no hairline mesh. The overall asymmetric silhouette should feel almost like a small typographic sculpture or woven seal, NOT a grid, not a hashtag, not a stock chart, not a letter monogram. Few parts, distinctive at small size. The viewer can discover code and candlesticks on closer inspection. All one flat warm copper-orange #ed9b6d, no outlines.
At right use exact words "budget terminal" in a strong, elegant lowercase contemporary sans-serif, medium weight with subtly softened human letterforms, pale warm white #f0e8dc. One line wordmark, excellent custom spacing, contemporary software identity.
Uniform near-black navy #080b12 background, wide horizontal composition, ample padding, symbol and wordmark balanced, one logo only. Strict flat graphic solid fills with clean crisp vector-like contours. No arrow, no coin, no currency sign, no terminal box, no app tile, no literal computer, no ornamental frame, no literal code text outside symbol, no tiny chart labels, no slogan, no watermark, no mockup, no glow, no shadow, no texture, no gradient, no 3D. Distinctive founder-led software brand, not a generic fintech template.
```
