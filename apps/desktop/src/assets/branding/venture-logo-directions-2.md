# Additional investor presentation logo directions

Three additional concepts generated using the built-in image generation tool. Each PNG has an opaque dark background and incorporates code and candlestick details. Earlier assets are preserved. These concepts have not been applied to the app interface or operating-system icons.

## 1. Fold

[PNG](budget-terminal-option-7-fold.png)

Final generation prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create one original sophisticated logo for Budget Terminal, a financial intelligence software startup. It should be memorable on an investor presentation cover and recognizable as a small app symbol.
Concept: a single folded-paper silhouette in luminous pale blue #b5d7ff, with deep cobalt #356aff flat facets, forming an abstract swift bird pointing sideways, not upward, made from only three broad angular planes. Its folded spine incorporates three small financial candlestick-shaped negative-space slots with proper thin wicks. A short pair of source-code bracket cuts and two staggered horizontal code-line incisions are integrated into one wing. Treat these as crafted structural details, not chart clipart pasted on. No literal animal details or feathers, no eyes. Strong compact silhouette and intelligent negative space.
At right exact text "Budget Terminal" in a distinctive contemporary serif wordmark, refined medium weight, elegant yet technological, ivory #f0f0e8. One line, generous space between symbol and name. Uniform near-black navy #080b12 background, wide horizontal canvas, ample padding, balanced scale. Finished identity, crisp flat vector-style solid fills.
No arrows, currency signs, coins, screens, rounded app tiles, generic infinity knots, BT monograms, slogans, labels, watermark, mockup, texture, gradients, glow, shadows or 3D. Only one emblem and wordmark. Prioritize restraint and originality.
```
