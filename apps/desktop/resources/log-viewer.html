What is recorded

Startup and shutdown, page readiness, service timing and freshness, classified errors, renderer failures, process exits and main-process memory.

Request and response payloads, chat text, holdings, browser addresses, credentials and raw error messages are excluded.

Storage is bounded to approximately 64 MiB and 15 days, with 4 MiB file rotation. Export includes the retained snapshot, including records hidden by filters.

Logs show observed symptoms. A healthy log does not prove every feature is working. Abrupt termination may have no final shutdown record.

Logs are local. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
