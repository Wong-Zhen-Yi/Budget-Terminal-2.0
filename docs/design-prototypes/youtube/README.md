# YouTube redesign prototypes

Static design concepts for Budget Terminal 2.0, generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. All video content and counts are fictional samples. Application code and behavior are unchanged.

- [01 — Research Terminal](01-research-terminal.png): dense thumbnail table and a right-side inspector.
- [02 — Video Library](02-video-library.png): thumbnail card grid, ticker filters and a video details panel.
- [03 — Focus Workspace](03-focus-workspace.png): large selected-video preview with a compact feed queue.

All three PNGs are 1672 × 941 pixels. Generated interface text and sample metadata are illustrative; final implementation should use the real data contracts and preserve startup readiness and visible failure states.

## Generation prompts

### Concept 1

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: a high-fidelity PNG desktop application redesign prototype for the YouTube page inside Budget Terminal 2.0, a stock portfolio research app.
Generate a SINGLE complete landscape screen, 1920x1080 composition, straight-on flat UI screenshot, crisp legible typography, pixel-aligned grids, production-quality interface design. This is a design prototype, with fictional sample videos and counts, not live market information.

A compact secondary breadcrumb/navigation row with "Overview / YouTube" and tabs "Dashboard", "News", "Calendar", "YouTube", "Dictionary", YouTube selected. Keep shell visually subordinate to the redesign. Use modern sans-serif interface text, subtle borders, clean vector icons, accessible contrast.
Retain the actual page's purpose and data: recent YouTube videos for saved portfolio tickers, minimum 1,000 views, last 90 days; ticker, video title, channel, views, publication age and duration; filter by ticker/title/channel; sorting and Refresh control; clear data status and external "Watch on YouTube" action. Only use public ticker symbols NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, SOFI as fictional demo holdings and invented channels "Market Lens", "Equity Brief", "The Research Desk". Do not add AI summaries, sentiment scores, transcripts, buy/sell recommendations or features requiring unavailable video analysis. Thumbnails may use tasteful fictional editorial images of chips, corporate campus architecture, clean technology products and simple finance graphics, with minimal readable titles. No generic landing-page marketing, no device frame, no perspective, no surrounding desk, no watermarks, no giant logos. Every panel must read as a cohesive implementable app interface. Include a tiny unobtrusive footer "DESIGN PROTOTYPE · SAMPLE CONTENT".

Direction 01: "Research Terminal". A restrained charcoal and slate palette (#0B0F16 background, #111925 panels) with cool blue selection and small red YouTube mark. A substantial page title "YouTube" with muted subtitle "Research videos for your portfolio". Full-width clean toolbar with search field, ticker filter pills "All tickers", "NVDA", "AAPL", "MSFT", "GOOGL", "AMZN", a compact "Newest first" sorter and "Refresh" on the right. Small unobtrusive status line "43 videos · 6 tickers · Updated just now" plus "All pages ready" green dot. Main content takes most of viewport: approximately 68% width left research table, 32% right selected-video inspector. Design a beautifully aligned dense but legible table with column labels "TICKER", "VIDEO", "CHANNEL", "VIEWS", "PUBLISHED", "LENGTH", about 11 rows, 48px tall. Give every video row a small 16:9 thumbnail, bold readable video title, ticker pill and secondary channel text, active row slate blue with narrow blue left edge; avoid redundant overly small columns. Main selected title "NVIDIA earnings: what matters next"; other titles "Apple's next growth cycle", "Microsoft cloud: the quarterly picture", "Alphabet's quarter in context", "Amazon margins under the microscope", "SoFi: a closer look at the numbers". Right inspector: large 16:9 cinematic chip thumbnail with play overlay, ticker pill NVDA, selected title, channel Market Lens, publication age and view count, full-width blue button "Watch on YouTube" with external arrow; neatly spaced two-column metadata; section "Description" with short plausible channel-provided description. Bottom has subtle feed coverage strip "Last 90 days · 1K+ views". Minimal rounded corners 6px, sharp professional terminal feel, generous enough spacing to avoid cramped existing UI. Concept number "01 / RESEARCH TERMINAL" in tiny type beside title.
```
