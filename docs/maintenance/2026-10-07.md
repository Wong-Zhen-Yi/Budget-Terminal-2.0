## Data and persistence fixes

- Portfolio and Personal Finance distinguish unavailable marks from zero. Missing quotes, unsupported/missing currency conversion and unavailable option marks no longer produce fabricated market values, cost-basis substitutes or healthy-looking totals. Negative net worth/debt remains signed; incomplete marks/FX prevent misleading goal calculations.
- Source health preserves source observations, timestamps, stale/failure state and restart/backoff behavior. Retrieval time is distinguished from source observation time; cached composite results retain their oldest dependency observation. Quote/FX validation and partial-source failures propagate to consumers.
- Economic final-demand PPI now uses `PPIFID`, replacing the all-commodities `PPIACO` series. The series is monthly, not seasonally adjusted, and indexed to November 2009 = 100. FRED's BLS-sourced August 2026 observation was **157.604**, matching the raw live observation reviewed. The chart's calculation/label must reflect whether it displays the raw index or a derived change. [FRED PPIFID definition and observation](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/PPIFID).
- Treasury blank/NA fields remain unavailable instead of becoming zero. Supplemental independent CSV checks verify the valuation yield anchors at displayed precision.
- Oil EIA failures are observed per series and surface a partial envelope; transient 500/502/503/504 responses receive a bounded retry. Gold/Oil fallbacks carry source-health observations instead of silently resetting failure state.
- SEC debt normalization now chooses component debt **per statement date**, preserving a reported aggregate on dates without complete components. Annual and quarterly regressions cover mixed coverage and avoid discarding an entire reported series because another date has components.
- Simultaneous SEC requests for the same cache document coalesce. Atomic replacement retries transient Windows EPERM/EACCES/EBUSY locks five times, without deleting the previous destination. If durable caching still fails, fetched data remains usable with a local-cache warning. Regression cases cover concurrency, preserved old cache, transient/permanent failures and bounded retry. VST passed in the final full live run.
- Automatic-backup probes cover concurrent edits/exports, import/clear, rejected local saves, backup-write failure preservation and recovery, and preservation of the previous backup at startup.
- Market and renderer caches use schema version 3; Economic, Treasury, Spark and option-mark entries also invalidate incompatible previous formats. Nested envelopes propagate their complete health into parent calculations and caches. A derived cache cannot extend the earliest dependency expiry; coalesced requests retain their source health.
- Treasury and Money disk/memo reads preserve original collection and observation times. Failed current-year Treasury downloads and daily observations older than seven days remain explicit. Spark's initial disk write now retains its 120-second expiry.
- Option marks retain Yahoo's contract `lastTradeDate`. The table labels the value **Last trade**, with source/date/age tooltip; trades older than seven days remain usable but stale. Zero-valued valid trades remain zero. Missing marks remain N/A.
- EUR/GBp stock and chart quote headers display native units without guessing a conversion; USD formatting is preserved. USD portfolio/finance books withhold foreign marks when a required conversion is unavailable, and foreign chart prices are not compared against USD cost basis.
- Footer, inline quote/history labels and Calendar cache ages update from elapsed time using the shared TTL policy. Copied SOFI panel envelopes retain explicit TTL, query errors and enabled state. The clock does not request data or change the manual refresh policy for expensive feeds.

These are targeted corrections supported by regression evidence. Live HTTP restrictions remain visible; they are not converted into ready or successful pages.
