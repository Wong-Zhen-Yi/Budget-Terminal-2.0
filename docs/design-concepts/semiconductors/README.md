# Semiconductor page design concepts

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. All financial values and dates in the mockups are illustrative. These PNGs explore layout, not implemented features; generated metric labels may need reconciliation with the existing data contract during implementation.

1. Overview: peer performance, company table, company research, earnings and news in a balanced dashboard.
2. Company workbench: table-first screening with a persistent company inspector and comparison tray.
3. Supply chain: subsector browsing followed by filtered companies, performance and research.

## Generation prompts

### 01-overview

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished, realistic high fidelity PNG design concept for the Semiconductors page of Budget Terminal 2.0, a desktop financial research app. Wide landscape 16:10 approximately 1920x1200. Flat front-on screenshot, no device frame, no perspective, no decorative illustrations. Dark navy #080b12 background, subtly lighter #101722 panels, fine slate borders, blue #2f6fd0 accent, white and muted slate text, green positive and red negative numbers. Segoe UI style typography with aligned monospace financial values. Crisp readable labels, compact but comfortable spacing, professional information-dense terminal. Slim top app bar 'BUDGET TERMINAL' with global search. Left navigation 180px: Workspaces, Markets, Research, Portfolio; Favorites: Stocks, Charts, Semiconductors selected. Page title 'Semiconductors' subtitle 'U.S. supply chain'. Header refresh button and status 'Cached demo snapshot • 76/79 quotes'. Tiny footer 'DESIGN CONCEPT • Illustrative data' so no claim of live financial accuracy. Existing features to retain through organization: universe search/filter, market cap, 1D/1M/YTD returns, P/E, revenue growth, gross margin, selected company research and adjusted price chart, up-to-four-company comparison, earnings estimates, industry news, per-company data freshness. Eight subsectors are Chip design, Integrated manufacturers, Foundries, Memory, Equipment, Test & packaging, Design software & IP, Materials & suppliers. US catalog examples NVDA, AMD, AVGO, QCOM, INTC, GFS, MU, AMAT, LRCX, KLAC, AMKR, TER, CDNS, SNPS, ENTG. Use only illustrative values and fictional neutral news headlines. Prefer strong hierarchy and legible chart axes. Avoid oversized typography, gradients, glow, marketing hero area, 3D chips, arbitrary unsupported scores or signals. Direction 1: OVERVIEW WORKSPACE. A balanced dashboard designed for immediate industry scanning. Four compact KPI tiles in one row: Reviewed companies 79, Combined market cap $8.42T, Daily breadth 48 up / 28 down, Equal-weight return +0.84%. Next slim horizontal subsector selector with 8 compact pills. Main two-column area: left 68% width top is large 'Peer performance • adjusted returns' multi-line chart with NVDA AMD AVGO MU color chips, range tabs 1M 3M YTD 1Y, clean percentage axis and dates. Below is compact 'Company universe' searchable table with 7 visible rows and 8 columns, selected NVDA row blue, one MU data status marked amber Cached. Right 32% width top is 'NVDA • NVIDIA' research card with quoted demo price, day change, 6 metrics, two lines rationale and Stocks / Charts / Fundamentals buttons. Below right is Upcoming earnings with 3 entries then Industry news with 3 headlines. Balanced bento grid, small 6px corner radii. Top internal tabs 'Overview' selected, 'Companies', 'Compare', 'News & earnings'. All visible content fits one desktop viewport. Include a small NVDA adjusted price sparkline in research card. This concept puts overview and comparison above company screening.
